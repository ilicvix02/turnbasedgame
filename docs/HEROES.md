## Aurelian Throne

### <a id="aurel"></a>Aurel — *the Sun Throne*
★★★★★ · Radiant Knight · bruiser · archetypes: Dominion Control, Revive Sustain

> Health-scaling titan who ascends and seals all buffs and debuffs in a Sanctum.

- **S1 · Solar Edict** — No cooldown, +1 soul
  Attacks with damage proportional to max Health with a 75% chance to Decrease Defense for 1 turn. While Ascendant, deals additional damage equal to 7% of max Health.
- **S2 · Gilded Charge** — Cooldown 2, +1 soul
  Attacks all enemies with damage proportional to max Health. While Ascendant, also grants the caster a Barrier equal to 15% of max Health for 2 turns.
- **S3 · Throne of the Sun** — Cooldown 7, +3 soul
  Ascends for 3 turns and fully recovers Health. Creates the Dominion "Sanctum of Judgment" for 2 turns: no buffs or debuffs can be applied to anyone. Starts battle on cooldown.

*Imprint concentration:* HP · *Lore:* Once a shepherd, Aurel was struck by a falling sunbeam and stood up wearing it like armor. The Aurelian Throne crowned him because no one could find a crown heavy enough to keep him down.

### <a id="seraphine"></a>Seraphine — *the Lumen Choir*
★★★★★ · Radiant Soul Weaver · healer · archetypes: Revive Sustain

> Revives the fallen with Lumen; the pillar of every sustain team.

- **S1 · Lumen Strike** — No cooldown, +1 soul
  Attacks with damage proportional to max Health, then heals all allies by 5% of the caster's max Health.
  *Soul Burn (10):* Also removes 2 debuffs from the ally with the most debuffs and increases their Combat Readiness by 20%.
- **S2 · Vigil of Lumen** — Passive
  Begins battle with 1 Lumen (2 if Aurel is an ally). When another ally dies, consumes 1 Lumen to revive them at 30% Health with +15% Combat Readiness. While holding Lumen, the first debuff inflicted on the caster each turn is purged and max Health is increased by 5% per Lumen.
- **S3 · Dawnchorus** — Cooldown 7, +2 soul
  Removes all debuffs from all allies, heals them by 22% of the caster's max Health and grants Increased Speed for 2 turns. If the caster has no Lumen, restores 1 Lumen (once per battle).

*Imprint release:* HP · *Lore:* The youngest Choir-Mother in Solmere's history, Seraphine carries three motes of the first dawn in her voice. Every note she sings is a promise that no one dies on her watch — a promise she has kept, at terrible cost to herself.

### <a id="verity"></a>Verity — *Lady of the Scales*
★★★★★ · Umbral Soul Weaver · support · archetypes: Revive Sustain, Dominion Control

> Fears, heals from the enemy's Health, and steals tempo whenever the enemy is pushed.

- **S1 · Weighed and Found** — No cooldown, +1 soul
  Attacks with a 75% chance to inflict Fear for 1 turn, then heals all allies by 3% of the target's max Health.
  *Soul Burn (10):* Attacks all enemies instead, inflicting Fear with 100% chance.
- **S2 · Equilibrium** — Passive
  Whenever an enemy gains Combat Readiness from an effect, the caster gains 50% of that amount (max 25% per turn).
- **S3 · Scales of Mercy** — Cooldown 5, +3 soul
  Removes all debuffs from all allies and increases their Combat Readiness by 25%. Then Tithe: the ally with the highest current Health loses 15% of it, and all other allies gain Bloom for 2 turns.

*Imprint concentration:* SPD · *Lore:* Verity was a court accountant in the Umbral Court who audited a god and found it in debt. The Aurelian Throne took her in; nobody in either court wants to owe her anything.
