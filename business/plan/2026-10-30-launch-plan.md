# Launch plan: done-for-you job costing for US plumbers and electricians (run from Serbia)

## Offer
"Your job costing and cash flow, set up in 48 hours." A custom version of our tracker for each client: their job types, truck stock, labor rates and logo. Works in Excel or Google Sheets.
- **Founding-client price: $150 for the first 5 clients** in exchange for honest feedback. This limit is real: after 5 clients it ends.
- **Then $250.** Optional $49/month: we update their sheet from receipts they forward and send a one-page monthly profit summary.

## Market
- **Who:** US plumbing and electrical companies with 1–15 people. Big enough to have jobs and trucks, small enough to have no accountant tracking job profit.
- **First metros:** Dallas–Fort Worth (TX) and Phoenix (AZ). Both are large and growing, with many small contractors.
- **Language:** English. Calls in US business hours, about 15:00–24:00 Serbian time.

## Getting paid (from Serbia)
| Channel | Use | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Gumroad | $49 PRO template + $150/$250 setup as a product checkout link | Bank payouts to Serbia in RSD, min 3,000 RSD. Owner must live in Serbia and verify ID. Gumroad handles US sales tax. |
| PayPal | Backup invoices | Receiving from abroad works in Serbia. Fees are higher, and the withdrawal route needs testing. |
| Upwork / Fiverr | Second client channel | Their own payout methods. Owner sets up payouts. |
| Stripe | none | Not available for Serbia-based businesses. |

Tax: income from foreign clients is taxable in Serbia. Ask a local accountant (knjigovođa) whether to register as a freelancer under the 2023 rules or as a preduzetnik before income starts.

## Daily routine (owner: ~45 min, Serbian evening)
1. 5 min: approve today's email batch (`APPROVE <ID>` or standing approval).
2. 10 min: read interested replies. Sales happen fully by email (no calls): sample → 3 questions → payment link → delivery.
3. 10 min: review a delivered sheet before it goes out.
