**Who it's for:** small US trade businesses (plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing…) with no website, an outdated one, or a builder template that doesn't bring in calls. Many of the companies we already researched fit this.

## How a project runs (all by email, no calls)
1. **Interest:** the prospect says YES to outreach.
2. **Discovery:** we send the questionnaire (`intake-questionnaire.md`). Follow-up questions until every required field is answered.
3. **Proposal:** a one-page scope with package, price, timeline, what's included and what isn't. The client replies "approved".
4. **Deposit:** 50% up front, 50% at launch (payment link or PayPal invoice, sent by the owner).
5. **Draft:** a private preview link of the full site with their real content.
6. **Revisions:** they reply with changes, numbered. We confirm the list back, apply it, and send a new preview. Rounds are counted.
7. **Launch:** deployed to the client's own free hosting account (Netlify or Cloudflare Pages) on their domain, with step-by-step DNS help by email.
8. **Handoff:** final balance, then a handoff email with logins they own, how to request changes, and the care-plan offer.

## Rules
- Use only the client's real information: real reviews (copied with permission from Google, Yelp or Facebook), real license numbers, real photos or clearly licensed stock. Never invent reviews, awards, years in business or certifications.
- The client owns the domain, the hosting account and the files. We never hold a site hostage.
- Anything outside the scope (online booking systems, payments, logins, blogs with 20 posts) gets a separate quote.
