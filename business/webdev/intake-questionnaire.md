# Website questionnaire (sent after a YES)

Send it as a numbered email, not a form, so the client can reply inline. Required items are marked ★. If an answer is vague, ask one specific follow-up instead of guessing.

**Intro to send above it:**
> Thanks! To build a site that actually brings you work, I need to know your business the way your best customer does. Answer what you can, and skip anything you're unsure of. I'll follow up on the important ones. Photos and logos can be attached to your reply.

## A. Business basics
1. ★ Business name exactly as it should appear, and any "doing business as" name
2. ★ Phone number for the site (the one that should ring), and text-message capable? (yes/no)
3. ★ Email that quote requests should go to
4. ★ Business address, or service-area only (no public address)?
5. ★ License numbers to show (state license, master plumber/electrician #), plus bonded/insured status
6. Hours, and do you offer 24/7 emergency service?
7. Year started, number of trucks/techs (only if you want it shown)

## B. Services and customers
8. ★ Your services, ranked by what makes you the most money (e.g. water heaters, repipes, panel upgrades, EV chargers)
9. ★ Services you DON'T want calls for
10. ★ Residential, commercial, or both? New construction or service/repair?
11. ★ Cities and neighborhoods you serve (in order of priority) and how far you'll drive
12. Who is your ideal customer? (homeowners in older homes, property managers, builders…)
13. What do customers say when they choose you over a competitor?
14. Do you offer financing, free estimates, warranties or senior/military discounts?

## C. Your current online presence
15. Current website address (if any): what do you hate about it, and what (if anything) works?
16. ★ Google Business Profile link, plus Yelp, Facebook, Nextdoor, BBB
17. ★ Can we copy your best Google reviews onto the site, with the reviewer's first name and last initial?
18. Do you own your domain name? Where is it registered (GoDaddy, Google/Squarespace, Namecheap…)? Do you have the login?
19. Competitors' websites you like (and why), and ones you don't

## D. Look and feel
20. ★ Logo file (PNG/SVG/AI). No logo? Say so and we'll do a clean text logo.
21. Brand colors (truck wrap, uniforms, business cards). A photo of your truck is perfect.
22. ★ Photos: your team, trucks, finished jobs, before/after. Phone photos are fine. Licensed stock photos can fill gaps.
23. Style in three words (e.g. "clean, trustworthy, modern" or "bold, local, family")
24. Anything you never want on the site (stock photos of smiling models, certain colors…)

## E. What the site must do
25. ★ The #1 action a visitor should take: call, text, or fill the quote form?
26. Quote form fields you need (job type, address, photos upload, preferred time)
27. Need Spanish (or another language)?
28. Online booking tool you already use (Housecall Pro, Jobber, ServiceTitan)? We can link or embed its booking button.
29. Anything legal that must appear (warranty terms, license disclaimers)?

## F. Logistics
30. ★ Starter (1 page) or Pro (up to 6 pages)? Not sure? Tell us your budget and I'll recommend.
31. Deadline or reason for the timing (busy season, new truck wraps…)
32. Who approves the design, if not you?
