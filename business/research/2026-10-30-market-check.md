## Takeaways
- **Price validated:** buyers pay $36–$69. Our PRO at $49 launch / $69 regular sits mid-market.
- **Our differentiator:** competitors track one job per file or skip cash flow. Ours does many jobs, overhead-adjusted net margin and a 12-month cash-shortfall warning in one file. Lead every listing with **cash flow + true margin**.
- **Free competitors are software lead magnets** (Knowify, CrewCost, Projul). Their blogs rank for "free job costing template", so our free version has to be better than a single-job sheet. It is: 3 jobs plus an auto flag.
- **Recurring pain** (from [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/how-to-track-job-costs)): sheets nobody updates until the job ends, and equipment costs getting missed. Content angle: "a 2-minute weekly habit" plus the equipment category.
- **Etsy is a sales channel too.** Many sellers are there, which means there's buyer traffic with no content needed. Etsy listing fee is $0.20 per listing (the only non-zero cost). Recommend listing PRO on Etsy *and* Gumroad.

- Must be 18+ and use your full legal name. Bank payouts need identity verification; PayPal payouts don't need an ID upload.
