The problem

Busy all month. Not sure where the money went.

Most small shops judge a job by the bank balance. By the time the numbers show a loss, the job is closed and the money is gone. These are the three leaks we see most:

Plumbing

Parts leave the truck and never hit the invoice

Fittings, valves and supply lines get pulled from truck stock on every call. If they aren't tied to a job, you paid for them and the customer didn't.

Electrical

Labor runs past the bid without anyone noticing

Catch an overrun halfway through and you can still write a change order. Find it at the end and you just eat it.

Both

Final payments stuck behind inspections

The work is done, the materials are paid for, and the last check waits on an inspector. Profitable on paper, short on cash in the bank.

Questions

Straight answers.

Who builds it?

Viktor Ilic, the founder of TradeSheets. He works remotely from Serbia and handles everything by email, so you never have to schedule a call.

Do I need any special software?

No. It runs in Microsoft Excel (2010 or newer) or free Google Sheets. There are no macros, no add-ons and no logins.

I already use QuickBooks. Do I still need this?

QuickBooks tells you what the business made overall. This tells you what each job made while it's still running, and when cash will get tight. Many shops use both.

Where does my data live?

In your own file, on your computer or your Google Drive. We don't host it, and we don't keep a copy after delivery unless you sign up for monthly upkeep.

How much time does it take each week?

About ten minutes: type in the week's receipts and payments. The dashboard and cash flow update themselves.
