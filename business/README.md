# TradeSheets — a $0-capital, AI-run digital product business

The model: build high-utility spreadsheet tools once, sell them forever through a Merchant of Record (Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy handles VAT and sales tax), and get traffic from search-driven content instead of ads. AI agents do the research, building, copywriting and analysis. You make the accounts, approve the work and post.

## Product #1 (built, ready to sell)

**Contractor Job Cost & Cash Flow Tracker.** Small contractors have money and admin pain, and they search for exactly this ("job costing spreadsheet").

| File | Price | What it has |
|---|---|---|
| `products/ContractorJobCostTracker-FREE.xlsx` | $0 (lead magnet) | 3 jobs, cost/payment logs, budget % and OVER BUDGET flag, upgrade CTA |
| `products/ContractorJobCostTracker-PRO.xlsx` | $49 launch → $69 | 50 jobs, 1,000-row logs, cost by category, labor/materials variance, overhead allocation → net margin, billing gap, 12-month cash flow with CASH SHORTFALL warnings, change orders |

Both files are generated by `scripts/build_job_cost_tracker.py`. Every number is a live formula (0 errors after a LibreOffice recalc), and they work in Excel and Google Sheets with no macros. To regenerate: `python3 business/scripts/build_job_cost_tracker.py`.

## The agent team

The full org is 50 agents in 8 departments; see [ORG.md](ORG.md) for the hierarchy and how a week runs. The core five:

The files in `agents/` are Claude Code subagents. To enable them: `mkdir -p .claude/agents && cp business/agents/*.md .claude/agents/`. Then ask Claude, e.g., "use demand-scout to find product #2".

| Agent | Job | Output |
|---|---|---|
| `demand-scout` | Finds proven demand on Reddit, Etsy, Gumroad and YouTube, with real source links | `research/<date>-ideas.md` |
| `product-builder` | Turns an idea into a FREE + PRO spreadsheet build script and verifies it | `scripts/build_<slug>.py`, `products/*.xlsx` |
| `listing-writer` | Store copy, pricing, SEO tags | `marketing/<slug>-listing.md` |
| `content-engine` | A week of video scripts, value posts and drafted answers | `content/<date>-week.md` |
| `ops-analyst` | Weekly funnel and revenue review from your CSV exports, plus one decision | `metrics/<date>-review.md` |

**Weekly loop (about 3-4 hours of your time):** Monday, run `ops-analyst`, then `content-engine`. Tuesday to Friday, record 2 screen-share videos and post or answer on Reddit by hand. Once a month, run `demand-scout`. When a product passes $1,000 in profit, run `product-builder` → `listing-writer` for the next niche.

The agents draft, and you publish. Posting from your own accounts after reading the community's rules keeps you clear of spam bans. The marketing never uses fake reviews or fake accounts.

## Launch checklist (this week)

1. **Accounts (free):** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy, a YouTube channel, a Reddit account (start commenting helpfully now; new accounts get filtered).
2. **Listings:** create FREE and PRO from `marketing/gumroad-listing.md`. Upload the xlsx files, and add a Google Sheets "Make a copy" link if you want (upload to Drive → share → change `/edit` to `/copy`).
3. **Wire the upsell:** paste your PRO link into `Start Here!B21` of the FREE file, then re-upload it.
4. **Mockups:** screenshot the Job Dashboard and Cash Flow tabs and frame them on shots.so or in Canva.
5. **Traffic:** record videos 1 and 5 from `marketing/content-scripts.md`, and write Post A for one subreddit.
6. **Track:** export Gumroad sales and YouTube stats weekly into `business/metrics/` for `ops-analyst`.
